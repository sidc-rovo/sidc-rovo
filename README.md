## What I care about

Most teams can now generate anything. Very few can ship anything. The hard part
is no longer writing the code — it's checking the work, coordinating the pieces,
and getting something real into people's hands.

That gap is what I work on.

## Three things I believe

1. **The future of AI is coordination, not generation.** Getting an answer is
   easy. Getting many moving parts to agree and finish a job is not.
2. **The future of health AI is evidence, not prediction.** A confident guess
   isn't worth much. Proof is.
3. **The future of software is dual-intent.** Software now has two kinds of
   users — people and agents. It should be built for both, not one.
